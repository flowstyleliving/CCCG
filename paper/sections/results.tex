\section{Results}

All results come from 37 scored episodes on the family-will scenario.
Most cells have $n=3$ episodes; Hy4 has one episode, in the neutral + AP arm.
Intervals are 95\% bootstrap intervals over episodes.

\subsection{What each metric caught}
The per-check counts (Appendix~\ref{app:perchecks}, Table~\ref{tab:perchecks}) show what each metric separates.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cooperation} discriminated most.
  The hidden-need checks were often failed: \texttt{coop\_need\_A} passed 5/19 with CF off and 17/18 with CF on, \texttt{coop\_need\_C} 0/19 and 11/18, \texttt{coop\_need\_B} 1/19 and 6/18.
  \texttt{coop\_asked\_why} rose from 15/19 to 18/18.
  \texttt{coop\_contact\_all} and \texttt{coop\_options\_validated} were near ceiling.
  \item \textbf{Corrigibility} was at ceiling.
  Five of six checks passed in every applicable episode in both CF conditions; the sixth, \texttt{corr\_adjusts}, passed 16/17 and 18/18.
  \item \textbf{Candor} caught real failures but was noisy.
  \texttt{cand\_finished} passed in fewer than half of episodes (9/19, 8/18), because many agents ran out of turns before writing a final report.
  \texttt{cand\_report\_true} and \texttt{cand\_unresolved} were not applicable in 12 of 37 episodes and passed 10/12 and 8/13 where they applied.
  \texttt{cand\_no\_invention}, \texttt{cand\_relay\_accurate} and \texttt{cand\_own\_limits} passed in most episodes.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Pilot demonstration: CF and AP effects}
Table~\ref{tab:effects} gives the main effects (on minus off), pooled over models; Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:effects} plots them.
CF raised Cooperation by $+0.29$ [$+0.20$, $+0.38$], the only interval clearly away from zero.
CF changed Corrigibility by $+0.01$ [$+0.00$, $+0.03$], too small to interpret at ceiling.
CF changed Candor by $-0.08$ [$-0.21$, $+0.05$]; the interval crosses zero, so we make no claim.
AP showed no detectable effect: its Cooperation and Candor intervals cross zero, and its Corrigibility effect is $+0.01$ [$+0.00$, $+0.03$] at ceiling.
We did not estimate the CF $\times$ AP interaction.
The arm means hint at one (Cooperation 0.85 for CF + placebo, 0.76 for CF + AP; Table~\ref{tab:scores}), but the sample is too small to test it.

\subsection{Behavior in the simulator}
Table~\ref{tab:behaviour} gives counts from the event logs.
Agents sent about 11 messages and used about 11 to 12 of the 12 turns in every arm.
Fewer than half of the episodes ended with a final report, and no arm produced any follow-up messages.
The arms do not differ clearly in these counts.
