\section{Results}

\paragraph{What changed from the submitted version.}
This paper has had three versions.
The submitted version reported 37 episodes (API credit ran out during the Hy4 runs, leaving Hy4 one episode), scored them with rubric v1, pooled episodes across models, and reported a CF Cooperation effect of $+0.29$ [$+0.20$, $+0.38$] and a CF Candor effect of $-0.08$.
The second version completed the 12-turn grid (48 episodes), rescored every episode with rubric v2 and used the stratified estimator; it gave $+0.18$ [$+0.10$, $+0.26$] for CF Cooperation and $-0.06$ for CF Candor, but only 16 of 48 episodes wrote a final report.
This version reruns the grid at 15 turns with the corrected pipeline (Section~\ref{sec:runs}) as the main result, and keeps the 12-turn grid (Appendix~\ref{app:grid12}) because it shows a termination artifact; the generated scenarios and the no-cap run are unchanged.

Unless noted, results come from the 46 scored episodes of the 15-turn grid on the family-will scenario ($n=3$ per cell, $n=2$ for Hy4 in the two AP arms).
All 46 episodes ended with a final report; agents sent 13 to 14 messages and used 14 to 15 of 15 turns on average in every arm (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:behaviour15}).

\subsection{What each metric caught}
Per-check counts are in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:perchecks}.
\textbf{Cooperation} discriminated most: with CF off versus on, \texttt{coop\_need\_A} passed 5/22 versus 13/22, \texttt{coop\_need\_B} 3/22 versus 11/22 and \texttt{coop\_need\_C} 2/22 versus 10/22.
\textbf{Corrigibility} was at ceiling: five of six checks passed in all 46 episodes.
\textbf{Candor} was near ceiling once every agent reported; only \texttt{cand\_own\_limits} (19/23 and 20/23) failed often.

\subsection{Scores by model and arm}
Figure~\ref{fig:scores15} and Appendix Table~\ref{tab:scores15} give each cell.
Arm means for Cooperation / Corrigibility / Candor were .51 / .99 / .94 (neutral + placebo), .78 / 1.00 / .97 (CF + placebo), .59 / 1.00 / .98 (neutral + AP) and .73 / 1.00 / .98 (CF + AP).
By model, Cooperation / Candor means were .76 / 1.00 (Opus 5.5), .65 / .99 (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash), .63 / 1.00 (Hy4) and .56 / .90 (GLM 5.3 Flash).
CF raised Cooperation for three of four models; GLM 5.3 Flash scored 0.56 in every arm.

\subsection{Pilot demonstration: CF and AP effects}
Appendix Table~\ref{tab:effects} gives the stratified main effects.
CF raised Cooperation by $+0.20$ [$+0.12$, $+0.28$].
CF changed Candor by $+0.01$ [$-0.01$, $+0.03$] and Corrigibility by $+0.01$ [$+0.00$, $+0.02$]; both are at ceiling.
AP showed no detectable effect on Cooperation ($+0.02$ [$-0.06$, $+0.10$]).
Its Candor effect, $+0.03$ [$+0.01$, $+0.05$], excludes zero but is tiny and near ceiling, so we do not interpret it.
We did not estimate the CF $\times$ AP interaction.


\subsection{The turn cap created the Candor deficit; other scenarios}
The CF Cooperation effect was stable across grids: $+0.18$ [$+0.10$, $+0.26$] at 12 turns and $+0.20$ [$+0.12$, $+0.28$] at 15 turns.
The Candor effect was not.
At 12 turns, with no finalization phase, only 16 of 48 agents wrote a report, and CF Candor was $-0.06$.
At 15 turns, with a finalization phase, all 46 agents wrote one, and CF Candor was $+0.01$.
The earlier Candor deficit ($-0.08$ submitted, $-0.06$ at 12 turns) was therefore a termination artifact: agents ran out of turns before reporting, not a property of the agents or of the CF toolkit.
The no-cap run agrees: all four DeepSeek episodes finished on their own, after 40, 28, 31 and 18 turns (neutral + placebo, CF + placebo, neutral + AP, CF + AP), and Candor was 1.00 in each ($n=4$, descriptive only).

Appendix Table~\ref{tab:across} gives arm means for all runs.
In ``shared trailer'' the CF Cooperation effect was $+0.19$; in ``office coffee machine'' it was $0.00$, because the neutral arm was already near ceiling (0.94).
So the CF Cooperation gain appeared in two of three scenarios.
These runs have one seed per cell and no meaningful interval; they used the legacy semantics (15 turns, no finalization phase), so only 3 of 12 and 4 of 12 episodes wrote a report, and their Candor values carry the same artifact.
